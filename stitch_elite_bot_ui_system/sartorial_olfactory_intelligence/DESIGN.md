## Brand & Style

This design system merges the structured, confident tailoring of contemporary men’s editorial fashion with the dense taxonomy and connoisseurship of niche perfumery. Designed for discerning men, collectors, and fragrance aficionados, it emphasizes quiet mastery, tactile materiality, and intellectual depth over transient digital trends.

The aesthetic philosophy is grounded in **Editorial Minimalist Modernism** enriched by warm, organic nuances. It rejects disposable web gimmicks, high-saturation accents, and cyberpunk neon tropes. Instead, it relies on deep matte basalt foundations, razor-sharp architectural layout grids, rich tactile surfaces reminiscent of heavyweight card stock and burnished leather, and warm earth accents of terracotta and aged amber. Every component conveys deliberate curation, understated confidence, and refined sensory luxury.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for a tactile, low-fatigue dark experience that evokes luxury editorial prints and fragrance archives.

- **Canvas & Base Foundations:** The background operates on `#121316` (Deep Matte Charcoal), an unreflective, velvety dark plane that prevents eye strain and showcases product imagery with pure optical contrast.
- **Surface & Containers:** Secondary panels, catalog tiles, and accordion drawers utilize `#1A1C22` (Secondary Matte Container), paired with `#22252C` for interactive hovering states.
- **Structural Borders:** Delimited by `#282B34` (Subtle Slate Boundary), providing structural precision without abrasive, high-contrast grid lines.
- **Accents:** 
  - The primary accent is `#C85A32` (Terracotta Copper), representing earthy woods, leather goods, and warm distillation vessels. Used for high-intent CTAs, key taxonomy tags, and olfactory pyramid highlights.
  - The secondary accent is `#D97706` (Warm Amber), reserved for olfactory chord metrics, vintage indicators, and focused star ratings.
- **Typographic Hierarchy:** High-contrast text uses `#F1F2F5` (Creamy Off-White), an optical relief from harsh stark white. Muted metadata, olfactory chord bars, and secondary notes employ `#9CA3AF` (Refined Ash Gray) and `#6B7280` (Deep Caption Stone).

## Typography

Typography drives the editorial tension between masculine geometric tailoring and catalog precision.

- **Primary & Headline Font (Manrope):** Chosen for its balanced geometric proportions, modern humanist legibility, and architectural precision. It performs with equal elegance in uppercase collection titles and intimate scent evaluations.
- **Technical & Catalog Label Font (Space Grotesk):** Employed exclusively for classification indicators, concentration codes (e.g., EDP, Extrait, Tailored Fit), olfactory pyramid tiers, and navigation breadcrumbs. Its restrained mechanical structure echoes library archives and bespoke production tags.
- **Hierarchical Discipline:** All uppercase headings must carry wide letter-spacing (`0.12em`), while large display numbers and editorial deck copy pull slightly tighter (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to retain structural unity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a disciplined 12-column architectural grid on desktop, transitioning to an asymmetric 6-column grid on tablet, and a 4-column stack on mobile.

- **Vertical Cadence & Margins:** Desktop layouts anchor onto an outer margin of `margin-desktop` (3rem / 48px) with internal gutters of `gutter-lg` (2rem / 32px), allowing breathing room akin to broadsheet editorial spreads. Mobile interfaces adjust to `margin-mobile` (1rem / 16px) with tight gutters of `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px).
- **Asymmetric Editorial Weighting:** In catalog showcases and fragrance dossiers, content favors a 5:7 or 4:8 column split between high-resolution imagery and taxonomic breakdown (notes, performance data, accords).
- **Rhythm:** Spacing between related text lines rests on `space-xs` (4px) to `space-sm` (8px). Distinct sub-modules inside cards rely on `space-md` (16px), whereas modular sections breathe with `space-xl` (40px) or double-step increments.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids loud, fuzzy neon drop-shadows and glassmorphism distortions, prioritizing a tactile, layered matte finish:

- **Tonal Layering:** Depth is communicated through calibrated tonal tiers rather than z-index casting. Background canvas (`#121316`) serves as floor zero; cards and containers lift to `#1A1C22`; elevated flyout drawers and dropdown menus sit at `#22252C`.
- **Micro-Bevels and Ghost Outlines:** Every container tier is delimited by a 1px hairline border of `#282B34`. When focused or active, borders advance subtly to `#C85A32` at 40% opacity rather than emitting bright glows.
- **Ambient Matte Occlusion:** When popovers, accord menus, or modals must rise above the canvas, depth is provided by a localized, ultra-diffused shadow: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`, keeping the UI grounded in heavyweight material luxury.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined balance between architectural tailoring and ergonomic pills.

- **Base Form Factor:** Structural elements (cards, media frames, modal sheets, and data tables) use soft, measured geometry (`roundedness: 1`, 4px base border-radius). This mirrors the clean corners of bespoke fabric swatches and monolithic perfume packaging.
- **Pill Badging Exception:** Olfactory notes, concentration badges, seasonal indicators, and secondary filter tags explicitly adopt complete stadium/pill geometry (`border-radius: 9999px`). This deliberate contrast separates interactive metadata from the structural frame.
- **Proportions:** Aspect ratios for fragrance bottles and editorial portraiture follow strict `3:4` or `4:5` vertical rectangles, reinforcing traditional menswear lookbooks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Terracotta `#C85A32` fill, `#F1F2F5` text, 4px border radius. Padding: `10px 20px`. Font: Space Grotesk 12px, uppercase, letter spacing `0.08em`. Hover: Shifts to `#D7653C` with subtle scale transition (`ease-out 150ms`).
- **Secondary Action:** Outlined in 1px `#282B34` with an `#1A1C22` fill and `#F1F2F5` typography. Hover: Border illuminates to `#C85A32` (60% opacity), background shifts to `#22252C`.
- **Text & Editorial CTAs:** Borderless, Creamy `#F1F2F5` with an accompanying inline vector arrow and an animated terracotta underline that expands from left to right on hover.

### Chips & Olfactory Pills
- **Olfactory Accord Pills:** Stadium radius (`9999px`), 1px border `#282B34`, background `#1A1C22`, text `#9CA3AF`, 11px Space Grotesk.
- **Active / Selected Pill:** Background filled with `#C85A32` at 15% opacity, border `#C85A32`, text `#F1F2F5`.

### Accord Breakdown Bars (Olfactory Intelligence)
- Multi-tier horizontal stack displaying chord composition (e.g., Woody, Amber, Leather, Spice).
- Each tier is rendered as a clean, rounded-sm bar with graduated tonal fills (warm terracotta, deep amber, burnt umber, tobacco brown), accompanied by a right-aligned percentage in muted ash typography.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Background: `#16181E`, 1px border `#282B34`, 4px border radius.
- Text: `#F1F2F5`, placeholder `#6B7280`.
- Focus State: Border shifts to `#C85A32`, with no outer glow rings. Input height: 42px for catalog lookups.

### Cards & Editorial Grids
- **Fragrance Dossier & Lookbook Cards:** Built with `#1A1C22` matte background and 1px `#282B34` boundary. Images fill the top half on a neutral matte backdrop.
- Bottom padding: `space-lg` (24px).
- Information hierarchy: Uppercase house/brand label in `label-caps`, followed by fragrance title in `headline-sm`, completed by a pill-group of primary olfactory accords.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Selectors
- Checkboxes: 16x16px square with 2px corner radius, `#1A1C22` background, `#282B34` perimeter. When checked, filled with `#C85A32` featuring a crisp off-white check glyph.
- Size & Concentration Selectors: Segmented rectangular buttons with 4px radii, utilizing `#282B34` vertical dividers and instant active state swaps.

## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a dark cyber-luxe aesthetic engineered for precision price tracking, automated deal sniping, and luxury curation across men’s high fashion, grail sneakers, and niche fragrances. It conveys the authority and speed of algorithmic automation combined with the exclusivity of an invite-only private concierge.

### Visual Character
- **Dark Minimalist Cyber-Luxe:** Immersive obsidian and deep pitch canvas with translucent charcoal glassmorphism pods.
- **Surgical Precision:** Strict geometry, hair-thin architectural borders (1px translucent strokes), micro-data layouts, and high-frequency real-time signals.
- **High-Contrast Energy:** Pure brilliant white pill-shaped triggers contrasted against high-voltage electric neon accents for savings, price drops, and live arbitrage alerts.
- **Understated Sophistication:** Deep muted slate secondary text keeps cognitive load minimal while premium typography commands prestige.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-dark environment punctuated by targeted high-voltage accents and luminous interactive triggers.

### Core Swatches & Roles
- **Base Canvas (`#090A0F`):** Deep cosmic black foundation that recedes entirely into the background, providing maximum contrast for product imagery and floating overlays.
- **Glass Container Fill (`rgba(18, 21, 30, 0.75)`):** Smoked obsidian glass serving as the standard card and panel tier.
- **Border Stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`):** 1px structural definition dividing surfaces without visual heaviness.
- **Primary Accent (`#A3E635` - Neon Electric Lime):** Signals active savings, price sniper triggers, high-velocity drops, and positive delta percentages.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#10B981` - Cyber Emerald):** Used for verified in-stock indicators, low-risk volatility metrics, and authenticated retailer badges.
- **Primary Interactive (`#FFFFFF`):** Pill actions, hero prices, and active iconography.
- **Secondary Slate (`#94A3B8`):** Micro-labels, metadata timestamps, historical price trend labels, and inactive tab selectors.

## Typography

Typography blends the sculptural, geometric assertiveness of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and micro-data badges with the clean, highly legible utility of **Inter** for real-time comparative listings and body prose.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Tracked tight (-0.02em to -0.03em) to convey engineered density and technical luxury.
- **Numbers and Currency:** Always rendered using tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) in semi-bold or bold weights for smooth comparative vertical alignment.
- **Micro-Labels & Badges:** Uppercase or small-caps styling using `label-sm` with widened letter-spacing (+0.08em) for snipe targets, stock indicators, and SKU categorizations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a flexible 12-column modular grid with edge-to-edge dark spatial margins and tight, data-centric internal module gutters.

### Structure & Rhythm
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters and generous outer margins (`2rem` minimum, standard `3rem` on ultra-wide screens).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 24px (`1.5rem`) outer canvas inset.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) horizontal screen padding. Deal tiles collapse into horizontal snap-carousels or single-column stacked telemetry feeds.
- **Layout Rhythm:** Component interiors respect a strict 4px/8px modular base scale, grouping related telemetry points tightly (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) while isolating independent interactive cards with `space-md` and `space-lg`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glass tiers, hairline perimeter illumination, and chromatic neon drop-shadows rather than muddy black drop-shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Tier (Ground):** Canvas `#090A0F`. No blur, 0 elevation.
- **Container Level 1 (Product Cards, Feeds):** Background `rgba(18, 21, 30, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a continuous boundary stroke of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Container Level 2 (Hovered Cards, Dropdowns, Flyouts):** Background `rgba(24, 28, 40, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`, and a diffuse ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Interactive Action Hover:** Pure white pill buttons gain a radiant atmospheric aura: `0 0 24px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`.
- **Sniper / Best Deal Accent Glow:** Neon lime elements project an electric neon aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(163, 230, 53, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a high-contrast hybrid geometry: pill-shaped capsules for high-intent actions and status tags, paired with soft, structured geometric rectangles for containers and cards.

### Shape Syntax
- **Interactive Triggers & CTA Buttons:** Full pill contours (`border-radius: 9999px`) to match the signature hero purchase triggers seen in high-end streetwear and sportswear flagships.
- **Glass Containers & Module Pods:** Rounded-xl radii (`1.5rem` / `24px` on desktop, `1rem` / `16px` on mobile) to frame product showcases smoothly without feeling harsh.
- **Input Fields & Search Strips:** Pill or deeply rounded forms (`border-radius: 9999px` or `0.75rem`) providing a sleek, automotive cockpit dashboard aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (The White Pill):** Solid `#FFFFFF` fill, text `#090A0F` in bold `Plus Jakarta Sans`, full pill radius (`rounded-full`). Padding: `12px 28px`. Hover adds an ethereal white backlight glow (`0 0 20px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`), with active state slightly scaling down (98%).
- **Sniper Action Trigger:** Solid `#A3E635` fill with `#090A0F` bold typography for real-time automated execution (e.g., "Auto-Snipe Deal").
- **Secondary Ghost:** Transparent glass fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`), `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover elevates border to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)` and background to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

### Badges & Chips
- **Discount & Savings Tags:** Compact pill badges featuring `rgba(163, 230, 53, 0.12)` fill, `#A3E635` text, and a `1px solid rgba(163, 230, 53, 0.3)` border with micro-uppercase bold tracking.
- **Filter Chips:** Smoked charcoal pills (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`) with `#94A3B8` labels; selected state shifts to solid `#FFFFFF` pill with `#090A0F` text.

### Product & Deal Sniper Cards
- **Card Anatomy:** Smoked glass backing (`rgba(18, 21, 30, 0.75)`), subtle `1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` border, `24px` corner radii.
- **Imagery Stage:** Centered dark-stage product isolation with subtle radial ambient glow matching product tones behind high-fashion sneakers or luxury fragrance bottles.
- **Telemetry Row:** Historical lowest price vs. live sniper price highlighted with `#A3E635`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Floating obsidian pills (`rgba(14, 17, 24, 0.9)`) framed by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Focus state illuminates the border with `#A3E635` at 50% opacity and a subtle ambient halo. Placeholder text sits in `#94A3B8`.

### Checkboxes, Toggles & Radios
- **Toggle Switches:** Compact pill capsule with pitch black trough and a glowing white or `#A3E635` knob.
- **Selection Checks:** Squared-pill check frames with neon lime checkmark fills on active tracking alerts.

### Live Snipe Activity Feed (Specialized)
- Monospaced ticker elements (`JetBrains Mono` or tabular `Inter`) displaying real-time price drops across global boutiques, with pulsing neon status dots (`#A3E635` and `#10B981`) indicating active API webhooks.
